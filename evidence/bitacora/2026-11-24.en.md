# Log · November 24, 2026

## What happened

I ran the version campaign: repeating the checks of OpenAI's proofs on three Lean releases, with a plan registered before running (commit `8df301b`). The audited version, v4.34.0-rc2 (R0), passed everything, including lean4lean, the third kernel. With that, acceptance criterion AC-2.4 turns to yes and the audit stands at 20 of 24 criteria. On v4.34.0 (R1) and v4.35.0 (R2) the statements compile unchanged, but the proofs do not. The Linux host shut down twice during the night and I spent several hours unable to log in. While rerunning the traps I found a defect of mine in the published evidence: three mutant diffs are missing a line.

Times are UTC. The runs started at 00:42Z on the 24th, which in Colombia was still the night of the 23rd.

### The two shutdowns and the lockout

- **01:27:56Z.** The WSL virtual machine shut down. Every background job died: the R0 driver (running mutant `euler_tstar_half`), lean4lean on Euler and a first attempt at `domain_R3_to_periodic_data`. I relaunched them at about 01:38Z from step 9 of R0, with an idle session kept open to hold the VM up. The interrupted steps have no exit code and were run again from the start.
- **From about 02:00Z** the SSH server refused new logins for long stretches (`Exceeded MaxStartups`). R0 finished at 03:26:59Z. The R1 build started at 03:34:28Z and its log was last written at 03:40:05Z, with no exit code. The VM shut down again at 05:57:59Z, according to `journalctl`. I could log in again at 16:09Z.
- I then repeated the R1 build, continuing the partial `.lake` (16:10:28Z → 16:13:46Z, exit 1), and ran R2 (16:17Z → 16:24Z, exit 1). Everything is recorded in [INCIDENT.txt](../../experiments/version-port/R1/run/INCIDENT.txt).

None of this changed the published evidence: the data version is still `c8edd482375d` and the fingerprint `5292-A47D-AFA5`. It was a run of my own, on my own host and with AI assistance, so it is not an independent reproduction and does not change AC-5.4.
