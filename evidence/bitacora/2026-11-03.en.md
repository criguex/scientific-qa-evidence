# Log · November 3, 2026

## What happened

On October 27 I fixed the CI after an Ubuntu update. That fix touched `.github/workflows/verify.yml`, which was listed in `evidence/SHA256SUMS.txt`. The data version came from the hash of the whole manifest, so it went from `26791fe6546f` to `727c5b5f98ef` and the fingerprint from `C5F0-F1A8-2F4D` to `1A86-6B19-21C7`, even though the evidence was the same. The page kept comparing everything against `C5F0-F1A8-2F4D`. Because of this, anyone who had repeated it after October 27 would have seen their correct result marked as "different". I received no reports in those days.
